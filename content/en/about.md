hanage.app publishes browser games and web apps I develop on my own. They're free to play and don't require installation.

I handle planning, development, and updates after release myself.

Before building a game, I sometimes make a small test page to try the controls or rules. For Putt, I tested swipe speed. For Multicolor Sweeper, I compared playing with three and four bomb colors before deciding on the final setup.

I write about why I started each game and what I tried along the way in the [development notes](/en/notes/).
