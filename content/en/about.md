hanage.app is where I put the browser games and web apps I make on my own. Nothing to install, and they're free.

Planning, rules, screens, and tuning after release: I do all of it myself.

I usually don't start with the real thing. For Putt, I first built a page that only measured swipe speed. For Multicolor Sweeper, a page for playing three colors against four. I tried those, then decided the rules.

Why I made things, and the prototypes I dropped, go in the [notes](/en/notes/).
